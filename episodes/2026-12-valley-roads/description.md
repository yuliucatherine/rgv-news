Starbase Texas: 10x the Traffic on the Only Road In. Where Can the Valley Grow? What Builders and Investors Need to Know

The only road to Starbase went from about 645 vehicles a day in 2019 to more than 6,700 in 2024, and Texas just committed $192 million to widen it. South Padre Island still has one bridge in and out, and a second causeway could cost up to $1 billion. A $62 million upgrade is coming to the Donna bridge, but the truck corridor behind it is stuck, and the Valley's interstates still don't connect to the rest of the country. We break down which road projects are funded, which are still studies, and what that means if you build, buy or lend in the Rio Grande Valley.
