Texas Data Centers: SpaceX's $24B Starbase Plan Hits a Wall. What Builders & Investors Need to Know

Cameron County is looking at roughly $42 billion in data center plans: Fluidstack's $4 billion campus near Rio Hondo, already under construction; a proposed Eneus Energy campus near Harlingen, valued at up to $14 billion in a study the company commissioned; and SpaceX's $24 billion computing complex at Starbase. But Texas has hit the brakes. The governor froze state data center permits, the governor-elect has promised a moratorium, and Cameron County commissioners voted 3-2 not to move SpaceX's tax break forward. We break down what's being built, what's on hold, and the dates that decide it: Dec 9, mid-December, Jan 12 and Jan 19.
