Rio Grande Valley: $41B in Projects, 700 Jobs Lost. What Builders & Investors Need to Know

Cameron County counts more than $41 billion in just four projects, two LNG export terminals, SpaceX's Starbase and Saronic's Port Alpha shipyard. Yet the Dallas Fed says Rio Grande Valley employment fell at an annualized 0.6% in the third quarter, about 700 jobs. The McAllen metro added 4,500 jobs over the year while Brownsville lost 300, unemployment fell partly because the labor force shrank, and Valley wages are still the lowest in Texas. We break down where each big project really stands, why the paychecks lag, the cracks to watch (trucking bankruptcies and the screwworm quarantine), and what it means if you build, buy or lend in the Rio Grande Valley.
